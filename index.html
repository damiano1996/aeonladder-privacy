What the game stores

Aeon Ladder works offline. Your progress (best streaks, coins, unlocked packs, settings, language, daily history) is stored only on your device. We do not run a server, there are no accounts, and we do not receive this data. Uninstalling the app removes it.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or messages. The game has no analytics of its own and requests no internet permission.

Advertising

This version of the game does not show ads and does not use any advertising or analytics service.

Links and sharing

When you tap "Read on Wikipedia" or a licence link, the page opens in your browser; that site has its own privacy policy. When you share a result image, it is passed to the app you choose.

Content sources

Facts come from Wikidata (CC0) and Wikipedia summaries (CC BY-SA 4.0). Aeon Ladder is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wikipedia or the Wikimedia Foundation. Facts can contain errors.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights and contact

We do not hold personal data about you. For questions, or to exercise rights under the GDPR with respect to any data we might hold, write to aeonladder.games@gmail.com. Changes to this policy are posted at this address.
